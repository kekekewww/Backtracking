Notes
- IBM Plex carries the Reserved Font Name "Plex". These are Google Fonts' own subsets, used
  unmodified here. Do not re-subset or edit these files and keep the "Plex" name; if a custom
  subset is ever needed, start from IBM's release and check the RFN terms first.
- No CJK fonts are included yet. Traditional Chinese pairings are proposed in each X-notes.md.
- Subset does not include U+2190/U+2192 arrows. Use SVG for arrows.
