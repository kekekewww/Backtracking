How it works

10 s20 s30 s60 s3 min5 min10 min
  1. 1

    It's already listening.

    Backtracking keeps the last 10 seconds to 10 minutes of audio in memory.

  2. 2

    Select the part you want.

    Drag across the waveform. Hold Shift to snap to flags, bars or seconds.

  3. 3

    Drag it out as a WAV.

    Drop it on a DAW track or into a folder. Recording keeps running in the background.

Features

  • A/B

    Record two sources at once and save two aligned WAVs.

  • Snapshot shortcut

    Save from the background: Alt + ` on Windows, Fn/Globe + ` on macOS.

  • Waveform only

    Shrink the window to a thin waveform strip, with adjustable transparency, kept on top.

  • Sources

    Capture system playback, a microphone, or one chosen app.

  • Plug-in

    Insert it on any DAW track to capture audio at that point in the chain.

  • Meter

    Live level in dBFS, VU or momentary LUFS.

  • WAV export

    44.1, 48 or 96 kHz; 16-bit, 24-bit PCM or 32-bit float.

  • Keep quiet sections

    Optionally keep silence instead of compressing it.

  • Sender

    A plug-in instance can send its audio to the desktop recorder on the same computer.
